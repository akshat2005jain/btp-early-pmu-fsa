1. Engineering Motivation & Problem Statement

Why Low-Inertia 50.0 Hz Grids Need Sub-Second Pre-Nadir Screening

2 / 16

Physical Vulnerability Under Renewable Displacement

  • 50.0 Hz Synchronous Balance: Following a sudden generator trip at t0 = 1.0 s (80–1,200 MW), kinetic energy is immediately drained from online turbine rotors.
  • Zero-Inertia GFL Converters: Displacing 10%–50% of synchronous units with constant-PQ solar/wind converters (Hvir = 0) cuts system inertia Htot from 324.7 s down to 174.6 s.
  • Compressed Containment Margin: COI frequency plunges to fnadir within 1.78–2.70 s post-fault (tnadir ∈ [2.78, 3.70] s); breaching fsecure = 49.50 Hz risks emergency UFLS Stage-1 load shedding.

The Operational Screening Bottleneck

  • SCADA Is Too Slow: Polling every 2–4 s misses the entire primary arrest trajectory.
  • Time-Domain ODE Integration Is Heavy: Integrating 30 nonlinear swing + governor ODEs takes 125.38 ms median per 10 s run (>1 min for 500+ contingencies).
  • Full-Trajectory ML Gives Zero Warning: Observing post-nadir samples violates causality and leaves zero lead time for Fast Frequency Response (FFR).

Core Scientific Problem

Determine whether a strictly causal early window [t0, t0 + Tobs] (Tobs ∈ {0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0} s) of observable 50 fps wide-area PMU measurements suffices to predict continuous future fnadir and calibrated P(fnadir ≥ 49.50 Hz) with zero missed emergencies across unseen renewable/load regimes, noisy channels, and sparse PMU coverage.

8. Machine Learning & Probability Calibration Methodology

Seven Baselines + Physics-Guided Two-Stage Calibrated GBDT

9 / 16

7 Regressors & 7 Classifiers Benchmarked

  • Physics & Simple Baselines: Constant Mean, AnalyticalSwingNadirRegressor (quadratic + parabolic swing vertex −r0² / 2|c̄|), and StandardScaler + Ridge / Logistic Regression.
  • Non-Linear Tree Ensembles: CART Decision Tree (depth=5), Random Forest (100 trees), GBDT (100 trees, lr=0.08, depth=4), and LightGBM (HistGBDT, 15 leaves).
  • Physical Target Bounds: Continuous predictions bounded to [45.0, 50.5] Hz.

Proposed CalibratedGBDTClassifier

  • Stage 1 (Continuous Margin): Predicts continuous s(x) = f̂nadir(x) in Hz so 49.49 Hz and 48.26 Hz retain physical distance from 49.50 Hz.
  • Stage 2 (5-Fold Cross-Fit Calibration, Ncal=218):
    • Platt Scaling: Monotonic L-BFGS sigmoid (A = 71.81 > 0).
    • PAVA Isotonic: 216 non-parametric knots (fallback_used = False).
  • Stage 3 (OOD-Aware 95% PIs): Quantile GBDT (α=0.025, 0.975) + OOF conformal q95 + feature-space distance inflation.

15. Limitations, Future Roadmap & Team Viva Summary

Transparent Engineering Boundaries & Four-Member Contribution Map

16 / 16

Explicit Limitations

  • Research Prototype Only: Not certified for autonomous relay tripping without EMS/HIL qualification.
  • Classical 3rd-Order ODEs: Constant-flux E' & constant-Z Kron loads omit AVR/PSS, boiler limits, and EMT PLL dynamics.
  • Synthetic PMU Front-End: Additive Gaussian noise + OLS rather than sub-cycle DFT filters or bursty packet loss.
  • Tree OOD Tail Floor: Leaf predictions floor at 48.72 Hz on unseen 50% RES nadirs (48.26 Hz).

Future Research Extensions

  • Physics-Residual Hybrid Boosting: Fit GBDT residuals atop AnalyticalSwingNadirRegressor for exact OOD tail extrapolation.
  • Grid-Forming Converters: Virtual inertia (Hvir > 0), governor deadbands & turbine rate limiters.
  • Cyber-Resilient Sparse WAMS: Packet dropout imputation & N-2 line + generator contingencies.
  • OPAL-RT / RTDS HIL Testing: Live C37.118.2 UDP streaming.
